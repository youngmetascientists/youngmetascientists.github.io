title: "Meta Research Symposium 2026 + Travel Support Grants"

This year, PYMS is partnering with the Paul Meehl Graduate School (PMGS) to co-organize the **Meta Research Symposium 2026**, taking place on **8–9 October 2026** at **Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)**.

The two-day symposium will bring together early-career and senior meta-scientists for a diverse program of workshops, hackathons, and research presentations. For more information and to submit an abstract (**deadline: 15 July 2026**), visit the [PMGS website](https://paulmeehlschool.github.io/workshops/symposium2026/).

PYMS is also offering **Travel Support Grants** for early-career meta-scientists who would otherwise be unable to attend due to financial constraints. Applications for travel support can be submitted [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdjbc5WakxWioH6V-IQUKPI1p_VrnAnbqwdQL5R0b7s2JOu6g/viewform?usp=dialog).
